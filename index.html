Frequently Asked Questions

What services does ABC Research provide?

We provide comprehensive qualitative and quantitative research services tailored to ensure your product meets the highest standards of accessibility and user experience.

Are your color palettes WCAG compliant?

Yes, all color frameworks implemented in our showcases are carefully evaluated against strict WCAG contrast criteria to support low-vision and assistive technology users.

How can I participate in a playtest?

You can express your interest by navigating to our Playtest link in the top menu or by reaching out to us directly via the contact form above.

What is the typical turnaround time for a project?

Turnaround times vary depending on the depth and scope of the research requested, but our standard diagnostic cycles generally range between 2 to 4 weeks.
