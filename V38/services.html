Common Questions

What kinds of products do you research?

We research digital products such as games, websites, mobile apps, and software. The approach depends on the product, its stage of development, and the questions the study needs to answer.

How does a study work?

We start by defining the goals and research questions with the project team. Then we plan and run the study, analyze the results, and share the findings. Throughout the study, we keep the team informed and offer opportunities to stay involved.

What kind of deliverables can I expect?

We share findings in a format agreed on with the project team. Depending on the study, deliverables may include a report, recordings, transcripts, user quotes, or competitive comparisons. We can also include recommendations on request.

Is support available after the study?

We can answer questions and help interpret the findings after the study. If more research is needed, we can discuss follow-up studies.

Don’t see what you need?

Tell us what you’re trying to learn. We’ll help you choose a research approach that fits your goals.
