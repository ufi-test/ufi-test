Common questions

What kinds of products do you research?

We research digital products including games, websites, mobile apps and software. The approach depends on your product, its stage of development and the questions you need to answer.

How does a study work?

We start with your goals and research questions, plan and run the study, then analyze the findings and share recommendations. We provide progress updates by email and can discuss opportunities to observe sessions.

What will we receive?

You’ll receive a report explaining the findings and recommendations. Depending on the study, supporting materials can include recordings, transcripts, user quotes, design recommendations and competitive comparisons.

Is support available after the study?

We can answer questions and help you interpret the findings after a project. If you need additional research, we can discuss a follow-up study.

Don’t see what you need?

Tell us what you’re trying to learn. We can discuss other research approaches and whether we’re the right fit for your project.
