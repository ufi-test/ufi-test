Common questions

What kinds of products do you research?

We research digital products including games, websites, mobile apps, and software. The approach depends on your product, its stage of development, and the questions you need to answer.

How does a study work?

We start with your goals and research questions, plan and run the study, and then analyze and share the findings. We provide progress updates by email and can discuss opportunities to observe sessions.

What kind of deliverables can I expect?

You’ll receive research findings in a format agreed with you. Depending on the study, deliverables may include a report, recordings, transcripts, user quotes, or competitive comparisons. Recommendations can be included if you’d like them.

Is support available after the study?

We can answer questions and help you interpret the findings after a project. If you need additional research, we can discuss follow-up studies.

Don’t see what you need?

Tell us what you’re trying to learn. We can discuss other research approaches and whether we’re the right fit for your project.
