About UFi Research

Get to know us.

Research starts with people.

We believe understanding users should be a regular part of product development. Our goal is to help more teams make research part of how they build products.

We work with indie teams, studios, and startups to understand how people think, feel, and interact with their products. Our research approach is shaped by your goals, whether you’re refining an interface or exploring an early idea.

What guides our work

Empathy & curiosity

Understanding people’s needs and experiences is the starting point for our research.

Collaboration

Accessibility

We believe research can help uncover barriers that make products difficult to access, understand, or use.
