Research services

A clearer understanding of your users.

Start with the question.

Your goals shape the study. We work with you to choose an approach that fits your product and the decisions you need to make.

Playtesting

Where do players struggle, and what should we investigate next?

We plan a study around your game and research questions, recruit participants, and observe the player experience.

You receive: Findings about player behavior and experience, with recommendations to guide your next iteration.

Usability research

Can people complete the tasks your product needs to support?

We study how people navigate your interface and approach tasks, identifying confusing steps and friction along the way.

You receive: A report of usability findings and practical recommendations for improvement.

Expert analysis

Which usability concerns deserve closer attention?

We review your experience to identify potential usability issues and opportunities for further investigation.

You receive: An assessment of usability concerns and recommendations for what to investigate or improve. This review does not replace testing with users.
