Common questions

How do I join?

Fill out our participant signup form. When studies become available, we send out participation surveys so you can tell us more about yourself and your availability.

Does joining guarantee a study?

No. After you complete a participation survey, a researcher will reach out if you’re selected for that study.

Will I be compensated?

Yes. Participants are compensated for the studies they take part in. The amount varies depending on the type and length of the study.

Are all studies remote?

Many studies take place remotely using your computer or mobile device. If a study requires you to attend in person, you’ll know before signing up.

Can I share what I tested?

Study details and materials are confidential. Please do not share screenshots, recordings, or descriptions of the test. Participants must sign a non-disclosure agreement before testing.

Questions about taking part?

Contact our research team if you’d like to know more.
