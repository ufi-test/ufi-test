Download the article ZIP to upload to the website, or save an editable draft to send to your publisher. Nothing has been published.

How to upload the article
  1. Extract the ZIP.
  2. Merge the article HTML and assets folder into the website folder containing index.html. Keep the existing website files.
  3. Add the included homepage-link snippet to the homepage manually.
  4. Keep the editable-draft folder on your computer for future editing.

The ZIP includes these instructions. Finishing does not clear your article; you can return to editing at any time.

Writing your article

  1. Start with a template or a blank article. Enter your title and introduction.
  2. Point between sections and select + to add content there. Keyboard users can Tab to the same buttons. On touchscreens the buttons stay visible.
  3. Use Move up and Move down beside the drag handle to rearrange sections. Options contains copy and delete. Undo brings back recent changes.
  4. Inside a highlight box, add headings, text, or lists the same way.
  5. Use Save draft to keep a copy or send it to your publisher. Open that file later to continue editing.

Browser autosave is a convenience, not a backup. Use one editor tab at a time. Choose Finish article when you are ready to download or hand off your work. You can return to editing afterward.
